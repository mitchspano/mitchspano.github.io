# Arrive on Time

Ever been so focused on a task, or stuck in back-to-back meetings, that you
completely miss the notification for your next call? "Arrive on Time" prevents
those "oops, I'm late" moments. It's a simple, private, and secure browser
extension that automatically opens your Google Meet tab right before your
scheduled event, ensuring you arrive on time without breaking your focus.

## How It Works

"Arrive on Time" simplifies your meeting workflow with a secure, "set it and
forget it" approach:

1.  **Log in with Google**: The first time you use the extension, you'll be
    asked to log in to your Google account. This is a standard, secure process
    handled directly by Google.
2.  **Grant Calendar Permission**: The extension will ask for **read-only**
    permission to access your Google Calendar. This is the only permission it
    needs to function.
3.  **Automatic Monitoring**: Once authorized, the extension runs quietly in the
    background, periodically checking for upcoming events in your calendar.
4.  **Never Be Late Again**: Two minutes before a scheduled event, the extension
    will automatically open a new tab with the correct Google Meet link.

That's it! The extension handles everything for you automatically.

## Your Data and Your Privacy

We take your privacy very seriously. Here's exactly what our extension does with
your data and, more importantly, what it **does not** do.

### What We Use:

- **Read-Only Calendar Access**: The extension requires permission to view your
  calendar events. This is necessary to find the meeting links and their
  scheduled times. It cannot create, edit, or delete any events.
- **Authentication with Google**: We use Google's secure authentication system
  to confirm your identity. Your password and personal account details are never
  seen or stored by our extension.

### What We **DO NOT** Do:

- **No Data is Sent to Our Servers**: Your calendar information, including event
  details, attendees, and meeting links, **never leaves your computer**. All
  processing happens locally in your browser.
- **We Do Not Store Your Calendar Data**: The extension only looks for upcoming
  events in real-time. It does not save or log any information about your past
  or future meetings.
- **We Do Not Track Your Activity**: The extension does not track your browsing
  history or your activity on any website. Its sole function is to open Google
  Meet links at the appropriate time.

"Arrive on Time" is designed to be a simple and trustworthy utility to make your
day a little easier, with privacy at its core.
